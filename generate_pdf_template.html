1. Executive Description

Continuum IDR is a 100% offline Edge Navigation SDK that takes over the exact millisecond GPS drops, using only the motion sensors (accelerometer & gyroscope) already inside your smartphone. No wires, no car modifications, and zero internet required.

2. How It Works (Under the Hood)
Dual Edge ML Models (<140 µs Latency, <4.5 MB RAM)
A lightweight HistGradientBoosting model predicts driving speed from a 42-moment IMU window. A companion classifier triggers Zero-Velocity Updates (ZUPT) at traffic lights, locking speed to 0.0 m/s so your location doesn't drift while idling.
Tuned for Indian Roads & Two-Wheeler Dynamics
Filters harsh vertical shocks from potholes and speed breakers so road ruts aren't mistaken for braking. Decouples cornering roll-lean angles θ = arctan(v · ω / g) on two-wheelers to protect heading integrity.
Offline Road Snapping & Decentralized V2V Mesh
A localized spatial hash graph keeps your track centered on actual road vectors without cellular data, while an 18-byte peer-to-peer BLE mesh shares road hazards without internet connectivity.
★ The Killer Feature: Native Google Maps Integration
Continuum hooks directly into Android’s LocationManager.GPS_PROVIDER. Official, unmodified Google Maps continues navigating smoothly through tunnels as if satellites were still active. When you exit the tunnel, a 2.5-second blending filter prevents any sudden visual position jumping.
Real-World Dataset Validation
Tested and validated on the real-world IO-VNBD multi-driver driving dataset across 34 held-out blackout episodes, reducing median endpoint error by 28.7% over frozen GPS baselines.
3. Abstract

Continuum IDR is a 100% hardware-free, edge-native Inertial Dead-Reckoning navigation engine and reference application. It sustains continuous, turn-by-turn vehicle tracking during total GPS outages using exclusively the built-in 6-DoF accelerometer and gyroscope already present in standard consumer smartphones. The entire platform operates strictly on-device, requiring zero external wiring, zero vehicle modifications, and zero cellular internet connectivity.

4. Core Technical Architecture & Innovation
Dual Edge ML Kinematics
Traditional double-integration of raw acceleration causes explosive cubic position drift. Continuum replaces this with a dual-stage edge ML pipeline running at 10 Hz: a HistGradientBoosting regressor estimates forward vehicle velocity from rolling IMU statistics, while a LogisticRegression Zero-Velocity Update (ZUPT) classifier instantly halts velocity integration when the vehicle stops at signals or traffic jams. Both models are compiled into a zero-dependency Kotlin tree runner executing in <140 µs with <4.5 MB RAM.
Indian Road Surface Adaptation
Standard inertial algorithms falsely interpret vertical bumps as forward acceleration. Continuum incorporates an adaptive az vertical jerk filter to discard pothole and speed-breaker shocks. For two-wheelers, it decouples dynamic motorcycle lean angles θ = arctan(v · ω / g) to preserve accurate heading during aggressive turns.
Universal OS Mock GPS Relay
Synthesized coordinates are injected directly into Android’s LocationManager.GPS_PROVIDER. Any unmodified navigation app (Google Maps, Uber, Swiggy) immediately receives real-time positions without API modifications. Upon GPS signal restoration, a 2.5-second exponential filter smoothly blends the dead-reckoning trajectory back to satellite fixes, preventing visual snapping.
Decentralized BLE Mesh
Employs an 18-byte ad-hoc peer-to-peer BLE advertising beacon for vehicles to exchange offline hazard alerts and positioning confidence without cellular data.
5. Empirical Validation & Impact

Benchmarked on the open IO-VNBD real-world vehicle dataset across 34 GPS outage scenarios (10s to 60s durations, Driver E held-out split), Continuum IDR achieved a 28.7% reduction in median endpoint drift compared to classical kinematic integration. By converting standard smartphones into precision navigation nodes at zero cost, Continuum delivers enterprise-grade positioning resilience for gig fleets, emergency ambulances, and daily urban commuters.
